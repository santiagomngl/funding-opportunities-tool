### How much funding can the project receive?

**How much money can be requested?**

**CM2026-01 gives an indicative total project budget of €2–5 million, while NWO caps its contribution at €400,000 or €600,000 depending on the Dutch applicant structure.** The project budget includes funding from all participating funding organisations and any self-financing.

- **Indicative total project budget:** €2–5 million, including self-financing. This is a guideline rather than a binding minimum or maximum.
- **Maximum NWO request:** €400,000 with one national main applicant.
- **Higher NWO maximum:** €600,000 with a national main applicant and at least one national co-applicant, provided both request research-personnel costs.
- **Minimum NWO request:** No minimum stated.
- **Funding form:** Grant funding provided separately to eligible partners by their national or regional funding organisations.

**Who in the consortium receives the funding?**

**NWO funds eligible Dutch universities and research organisations at 100% but provides no funding to enterprises, public authorities or other ineligible organisation types under this route.**

| Organisation                                     | Funding rate |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -----------: |
| Eligible universities and research organisations |         100% |
| Large, medium and small enterprises              |   Not funded |
| Public authorities                               |   Not funded |
| Associations and NGOs                            |   Not funded |

- **TenneT participation:** TenneT cannot receive NWO funding and would need to participate as a self-financed consortium partner and, where applicable, as the Need Owner.
- **Self-financed costs:** TenneT’s contribution must be included in the total project budget but will not be reimbursed through the call.
- **Co-financing requirement:** NWO does not prescribe a co-financing percentage for organisations receiving its 100% funding. Other partners remain subject to their respective national funding rules.


**How should funding be distributed across the project budget?**

**The NWO budget must use the permitted budget modules and prescribed personnel rates.** The following limits materially affect budget preparation:

- **Personnel:** Postdoctoral positions, non-scientific personnel and research leave are eligible. A postdoctoral position must cover at least 12 months at a minimum of 0.5 FTE.
- **Research leave:** Maximum 10% of the NWO grant.
- **Material costs:** Maximum 25% of the NWO grant.
- **Third-party work:** Maximum 50% of the material-cost budget.
- **Knowledge utilisation:** A budget must be included; maximum 20% of the NWO grant.
- **Project management:** Maximum 5% of the NWO grant.
- **PhD positions:** Not eligible because the maximum project duration is three years.
- **Overhead:** Not eligible for NWO funding.
- **Personnel rates:** UNL, UMCNL or Government Tariff Manual rates apply, depending on the organisation. NWO may index personnel costs once when awarding the grant.


### What is the project start date?

**Project timing window**

**Projects must last no more than 36 months and should begin between September and December 2027.**

- **Maximum duration:** 36 months.
- **Earliest stated start:** 1 September 2027.
- **Latest required start:** 15 December 2027.
- **Exceptional later start:** Possible only with approval from the relevant funding organisations.

**Required conditions before start date**

**An NWO-funded project cannot start until all conditions in the grant decision have been satisfied.**

- **Consortium Agreement:** Must be signed by every consortium partner before the NWO-funded project starts.
- **National agreements:** Each funded partner concludes a separate funding agreement with its national or regional funding organisation.
- **Reporting:** NWO-funded partners must complete the required national scientific and financial reporting.



### How to submit a complete application?

**Application route**

**The call has two mandatory application stages.** Proposals must be written in English, use the mandatory template and be submitted through the CETPartnership Submission Platform by the applicable deadline.

- **Pre-proposal:** **8 October 2026, 14:00 CEST** — maximum 10-page project description.
- **Stage 1 result:** Early January 2027.
- **Full-proposal stage opens:** **8 January 2027**.
- **Full proposal:** **11 March 2027, 14:00 CET** — invited consortia only; maximum 30-page project description.
- **Transnational selection outcome:** Mid-June 2027.
- **NWO funding decision:** **28 June 2027**.

**Pass or fail submission requirements**

**A proposal will proceed only if it is complete and satisfies the transnational, call-module and national requirements.** Direct submission of a full proposal without a Stage 1 invitation is not permitted.

- **Pre-proposal description:** Maximum 10 pages using the mandatory template.
- **Partner information:** Every invited partner must accept its platform invitation and complete the required administrative and budget information before submission.
- **Principal-investigator CVs:** Optional at pre-proposal stage.
- **NWO budget form:** Recommended at pre-proposal stage to verify cost eligibility and tariff use.
- **Ethics assessment:** Mandatory self-assessment through the submission platform.
- **Do No Significant Harm assessment:** Mandatory self-assessment through the submission platform.
- **Reporting work package:** The proposal must contain a dedicated and resourced work package titled “Reporting and Knowledge Community”.

**Mandatory additional documents**

**Invited consortia must replace the initial description with a complete 30-page proposal and provide the additional partner documentation.**

- **Full project description:** Maximum 30 pages using the mandatory full-proposal template.
- **Principal-investigator CVs:** Mandatory for every consortium partner.
- **Self-financed partners:** A Letter of Commitment is mandatory for each self-financed partner. TenneT would therefore need to provide one.
- **NWO budget form:** Mandatory by **11 March 2027, 14:00 CET**.
- **Changes between stages:** The coordinator cannot change. Changes to partners, budget or duration must be justified and approved by the affected funding organisations.

**Conditional eligibility assessment (when applying with research organizations)**

**A proposed national co-applicant that is not automatically eligible as a recognised research organisation must obtain NWO approval before submission.** This assessment does not make private or public limited companies eligible.

- **Pre-proposal assessment deadline:** **24 September 2026, 14:00 CEST**.
- **Co-applicant added at full-proposal stage:** **25 February 2027, 14:00 CET**.
- **Required documents:** Recent Chamber of Commerce extract; deed of establishment or current articles of association; latest annual accounts and audit report; and completed Research Organisation Statement.
- **Audit exception:** Organisations not legally required to obtain an audit must provide evidence of that exemption.
- **Previously assessed organisation:** Contact NWO well before the deadline to determine whether a new assessment is necessary.
- **Consequence:** NWO will not accept the organisation as a national co-applicant if the required documents are late.

**Evaluation Criteria**

**Proposals are assessed on Excellence, Impact, and Quality and efficiency of implementation at both stages.**

- **Scoring:** Each criterion receives 0–5 points.
- **Individual threshold:** At least 3 points for every criterion.
- **Overall threshold:** At least 10 of 15 points.
- **Excellence:** Clear objectives, ambition beyond the state of the art and a sound methodology.
- **Impact:** Credible outcomes, significant contribution, transnational added value and effective dissemination, exploitation and stakeholder involvement.
- **Implementation:** Effective work plan, appropriate resources, clear participant roles and sufficient consortium capacity. The full proposal must also address risks and justify the distribution of effort.
- **Final selection:** Passing the thresholds does not guarantee funding. Proposals are ranked within CM2026-01 and selected subject to the available budgets of the participating funding organisations.
